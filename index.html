Google Trends Scraper - Trending Now & Trend Duration

Reliable Google Trends data on the Apify platform, billed pay-per-event in USD: trending searches with estimated search volume, active trends vs. ended, trend duration, and keyword interest over time and by region.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC. "Google" and "Google Trends" are trademarks of Google LLC. This is an independent tool that reads publicly available Google Trends data; it is not an official Google product.

▶ Run it on Apify

What it does

  • Trending searches for 100+ countries and sub-regions (e.g. US-CA, FR-75), with estimated search volume.
  • Active trends vs. ended, plus trend duration: how long each trend has been running.
  • Interest over time and interest by region for keywords you provide.
  • Up to ~370 terms per run in large markets. No item cap.
  • A run that collects nothing is not charged at all.

Why this one

  • Reliability first. The Google Trends source is flaky; this Actor degrades gracefully. When part of a request is throttled, it returns the regions that responded and reports the ones that didn't, instead of failing the run.
  • Honest pricing. A small start fee plus a per-result fee that scales down with volume. Zero rows, zero cost.
  • Agent-ready. Exposed to AI agents through Apify's MCP interface.
